Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Purpose

Homelab Gmail Manager is a personal, non-commercial application for organizing Gmail messages.

Google User Data

With user authorization, the application accesses Gmail messages and labels to classify, organize, label, and archive emails according to user-defined rules.

Data Storage

Email data and authentication credentials are processed and stored locally within the user's self-hosted environment when needed for the application's operation.

Data Sharing

Gmail user data is not sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention

Locally stored data may be deleted by the user at any time.

Access Revocation

Users may revoke Google account access through their Google Account settings.
